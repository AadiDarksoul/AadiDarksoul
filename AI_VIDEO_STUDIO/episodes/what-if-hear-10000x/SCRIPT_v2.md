# What If You Could Hear 10,000x Better? — SCRIPT v2 (retention cut)

Style: approved cinematic stickman look (see `hook/`). Target length **8:00–8:20** (v1 was ~8:30; the flab is cut).
Narration ≈ 1,080 words. VO = narrator ("you" = Kai). Characters: **Kai** (teal hoodie, headphones), **Tara** (8, the kid next door), **Captain Osei** (rescue lead), **Dev** (Kai's friend), **Maya** (classmate).
**Language: US English** for all narration, dialogue and on-screen text (US spelling, feet/miles, Mom, etc.). Voices: narrator `en_US-joe-medium`, kids/boys `en_US-ryan-medium` (pitched), girls/whisper `en_US-lessac-medium`.

## What changed from v1, and why

| Change | Retention reason |
|---|---|
| **Flash-forward teaser at 0:17** (2 s of the rubble + hands on headphones) | Promises the climax. Viewers stay to reach the moment they've already seen. |
| **Headphone test at 0:45** (a tiny tick in the LEFT ear only) | Interactive moment in the danger zone (0:30–1:00), and it drives "did you hear it?" comments |
| **Re-hook line every ~60 s** ("That's the good part. Here's the price.") | Resets attention right where people usually drop |
| **The hook's whisper is paid off at 3:45** ("that's the voice from the beginning") | Closing loop #1 feels rewarding, and loop #2 (the flash-forward) is still open |
| **Direct plant: "Three short, two long. Remember it."** | Viewers feel smart at the climax, and it powers the Easter-egg comment challenge |
| **Relatable mini-scenes**: exam, crush's heartbeat, parents whispering | Shareable "that would be me" moments, and they suit Shorts cut-downs |
| **New cliffhanger**: something underground taps Tara's rhythm *back* | A much creepier, more comment-worthy question than v1's "machines do" |
| **CTA = binary comment choice + Easter-egg hunt**, no mid-video "subscribe" | Choices make commenting effortless, and a mid-video sub ask causes drop-off |
| **No dead outro**: the cliffhanger visual runs under the end screen | People keep watching into the end screen, which raises clicks to the next video |

**Open-loop ledger** (a question is always open):
1. 0:09 Who whispered your name? → closed 3:45
2. 0:17 Why does he take the headphones off, and where is he? → closed 5:35
3. 2:25 "Here's the price" → the cost escalates until 6:50
4. 4:05 "Remember the rhythm" → closed 6:05
5. 7:45 What tapped back? → Part 2

## THE SCRIPT

### 0:00 — HOOK (already rendered: `hook/out/hook_SDR.mp4`)
*Classroom. A kid's heart pounds in red rings.*
**Kid:** "It wasn't me."
**VO:** He says it wasn't him. His heart just told you… it was.
*Pull back through the walls: the whole city as sound rings.*
**VO:** And through three walls… someone just whispered your name.
*[SILENCE 0.8 s] [TITLE: WHAT IF YOU COULD HEAR 10,000× BETTER?]*

### 0:17 — FLASH-FORWARD (5 s)
*Smash cut: night, floodlights, rubble, dust. Close-up: Kai's hands on his headphones, trembling. A roar starts to build, then hard cut to black.*
**VO:** By the end of this week, you'll do the one thing you promised you'd never do.
*Beat.*
**VO:** But first… Monday.

### 0:24 — THE RULES ("Silence was never silent")
**VO:** Normal ears can hear down to about zero decibels. A mosquito, ten feet away, in a silent room.
**VO:** Yours go forty decibels lower. Ten thousand times quieter.
*Visual: dB ladder; a tiny stickman falls past "mosquito" to "YOU: −40".*
**VO:** And here's the wild part: your ears were already almost perfect. At their quietest, your eardrum moves less than the width of an atom.
**VO:** Push that ten thousand times further, and you find out silence was never silent.
*SFX: a soft endless hiss fades in.*
**VO:** That hiss is air. Trillions of air molecules hitting your eardrums, every second, forever.

### 0:45 — HEADPHONE TEST (interactive)
**VO:** Quick test. If you're wearing headphones, stay quiet for one second.
*[All sound drops. One tiny tick, LEFT ear only.]*
**VO:** Left ear? That's what a pin dropping across a stadium sounds like to Kai.
**VO:** Now imagine every sound on Earth arriving like that. All day. Every day.
**Dev:** "Bro, why do you never take those off?"
**Kai:** "The air is too loud."
**Dev:** "…The air."
**Kai:** "The air."

### 1:10 — AMAZING ("You hear everything")
**VO:** So what's life like at ten thousand times? Honestly? Kind of amazing.
**VO:** You know who's at the door before they knock. Mom drags her left heel. The delivery guy jogs. The landlord tiptoes. That's how you know rent's going up.
**VO:** In exams, you can hear every pencil in the room…
*Visual: 30 pencils, each scratching a different-colored ring.*
**VO:** …which is useless, because you can't tell a B from a D by sound. You tried.
**VO:** People type their passwords right next to you. Every key clicks a little differently. That one's real: researchers trained an AI to steal passwords just from typing sounds, with about 95% accuracy. You just… don't.
**VO:** You hear your parents whisper about your birthday present two rooms away. You practice your surprised face for a week.
**VO:** And lies. A lie is hard work. The heart speeds up before the words even come out.
**Mom:** "Did you finish your homework?"
**Little brother:** "Yes." *(SFX: thump-thump-THUMP)*
**Kai:** "…He didn't."
**Little brother:** "I hate you."
**VO:** Then one day, Maya from homeroom says hi…
*SFX: her heartbeat, quick and fluttery.*
**VO:** …and her heart skips. You're the only person on Earth who knows.
**Kai (robotic):** "Hello. Yes. Hi."
**VO:** Superpower. Zero game.

### 2:25 — RE-HOOK → AWFUL ("Your body is a factory")
**VO:** That's the good part. Here's the price.
*Audio switches to "inside Kai": wet, low, rhythmic.*
**VO:** The loudest thing you will ever hear… is you. Your heart is a drum. Your blood is a river behind your ears. Chewing cereal is an earthquake. And when you move your eyes…
*SFX: a tiny creak.*
**VO:** …you can hear them move.
**VO:** That sounds made up. It isn't. People with a rare inner-ear condition called superior canal dehiscence really can hear their own eyeballs move.
**VO:** And your ears got ten thousand times more sensitive, but your pain limit didn't. A siren is a punch. A dropped plate is a gunshot.
*Visual: a siren drawn as a jagged ring smashing through the frame; Kai folds over.*
**VO:** Worst of all, there's no such thing as a whisper.
**Classmate (far down the corridor, whispering):** "He's nice, but… why the headphones? It's kinda weird."
**VO:** You hear every word. So the headphones stay on. To everyone else, you're the weird kid who never takes them off.
**VO:** To you, they're the only door you can close.

### 3:35 — RE-HOOK → TARA ("The voice through the wall")
**VO:** But there's one sound you never block out. It comes at 10:14, every night.
*SFX through the wall by his bed: tap-tap-tap… tap… tap.*
**VO:** Tara. Eight years old. Lives next door. Figured out the weird kid can hear everything.
*Cross-section: Tara lying on her bed, tapping the wall with a spoon, whispering.*
**Tara (whisper, through the wall):** "Kai… you awake?"
**VO:** Yeah. That's the voice from the beginning. She's whispered your name through that wall every night for a year.
**Tara (at the mailboxes, next day):** "You flinched when I was *about* to pop my gum."
**Kai:** "Nobody can hear that."
**Tara:** "Then why'd you flinch?"
**VO:** Three short, two long. Every night. Remember that rhythm.
*Beat.*
**VO:** It's going to matter.

### 4:15 — THE QUAKE ("Seven seconds")
**VO:** Thursday. 3:12 a.m. You hear it before anyone on Earth.
*SFX: sub-bass groan, felt more than heard.*
**VO:** A groan from deep underground, like the planet clearing its throat. You have seven seconds.
*Kai rips the door open, bangs on his parents' door. A countdown on the HUD: 7… 6… 5…*
**Kai:** "OUT! EVERYBODY OUT!"
**VO:** Your family makes it to the street.
*[SILENCE 1.0 s, then the collapse at FULL volume: the loudest moment in the video.]*
**VO:** The building next door doesn't. Six floors. Forty-one people.
*Beat. Tara's window, dark.*
**VO:** And one of them is Tara.

### 4:55 — THE RESCUE ("The loudest place on Earth")
*Floodlights, drills, generators, every machine a huge amber ring.*
**Captain Osei:** "Thermal can't see through that much concrete. We dig blind."
**VO:** This part is real: after big earthquakes, rescuers call for total silence. Drills stop, engines stop, nobody talks, so they can listen for anyone tapping under the rubble.
**Captain Osei (megaphone):** "SILENCE! Everybody quiet!"
**VO:** But their "quiet" is still a stadium for you. Two hundred people breathing. Hearts. Engines ticking as they cool. You can't pick one sound out of all that.
**VO:** Not with the headphones on.

### 5:30 — CLIMAX ("Take them off")
*Same shot as the flash-forward: Kai's hands on the headphones (payoff of loop #2).*
**VO:** You know the rules. To find one sound, you have to let in every sound. And every time you do, it costs you.
**VO:** You take them off.
*Sound: EVERYTHING at once: a roaring 3D wall, then a visual white-out.*
**VO:** Forty-one heartbeats under the concrete. Some fast. Some slow. One getting slower.
*SFX: a thin, high ringing creeps in, the cost made audible.*
**VO:** Water dripping. Concrete settling. Your own blood, screaming. And underneath all of it…
*Everything drops away except:* **tap-tap-tap… tap… tap.**
**VO:** Three short. Two long.
**Kai:** "North corner. Twenty feet down. She's alive."
**Captain Osei:** "How could you possibly—"
**Kai:** "She's telling me she's awake."
*They dig. A small dusty hand holding a spoon. Tara is lifted out.*
**Tara (weak):** "I knew you'd hear me."
*Kai, for the first time ever, taps back on the stretcher: two soft taps.*

### 6:45 — THE COST ("Real silence")
**VO:** The ringing doesn't stop that night. Or the next.
**VO:** The doctor says it might fade. It might not.
**VO:** But for three days the hiss is gone. The air, the blood, the whispers, all gone. For the first time in your life, you hear what everyone else calls silence.
*[SILENCE: 2 full seconds; Kai lying on grass, eyes closed, smiling.]*
**VO:** It's… nice.

### 7:10 — IS IT REAL? (30 s, fast and visual)
**VO:** Could anyone really hear like this? Some animals get scarily close.
- Barn owls can catch a mouse in total darkness, by sound alone.
- Elephants pick up far-away rumbles partly through the soles of their feet.
- The greater wax moth hears sounds up to 300,000 hertz, about fifteen times higher than you can.
- The quietest room people have built, an anechoic chamber at Microsoft, sits at about **minus 20 decibels**. People inside start hearing their own heartbeat and blood within minutes.
**VO:** Kai lives at minus forty. Every second of his life.

### 7:40 — CLIFFHANGER ("Something tapped back")
**VO:** On the fourth day, your hearing comes back. That night, at 10:14…
*SFX through the hospital wall: tap-tap-tap… tap… tap. Tara, recovering in the next room.*
**VO:** …Tara taps goodnight.
*Kai smiles. Then his smile freezes.*
*SFX from far, far below: tap-tap-tap… tap… tap.* (Same rhythm. Deeper. Metallic.)
**VO:** It came from under the city. Six hundred feet down.
**VO:** Something down there heard her too.
*Hard cut to black.*

### 7:58 — CTA (over the end-screen visual: city cross-section, a red ring slowly pulsing far underground)
**VO:** Would you take this power? If yes, comment **"I HEAR YOU."** If it's a no, comment **"TOO LOUD."**
**VO:** And Tara's rhythm is hidden three times before the earthquake. First person to timestamp all three gets pinned.
**VO:** Part 2 is coming, and only subscribers will hear it first.
*(End screen elements: "Part 2" placeholder / best previous video, Subscribe. The underground pulse keeps beating until the last frame.)*
